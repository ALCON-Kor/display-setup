Display Setup Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-12 · ALCON Co., Ltd.

Display Setup (for iPhone, iPad and Mac; the "App") by ALCON Co., Ltd. (the "Company") configures DM3 LED displays over Bluetooth. This policy applies to the App.

Personal information we collect

The App does not collect personal information. It has no account or sign-in, does not send information to servers of the Company or any third party, and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored only on your device

The App keeps the following information, needed for its features, only on your device (in the App's data folder). The Company cannot access it.

  • Your app language and appearance settings
  • The identifier and name of the display you last connected to, and remembered display names
  • The message language you last used, message text saved to your display, and messages not yet saved

On iPhone and iPad, deleting the App deletes this information. On Mac, this information may remain after you move the App to the Trash. To remove it, quit the App, choose Go > Go to Folder… in the Finder, open ~/Library/Containers, and move the Display Setup folder (com.alconint.displaysetup) to the Trash.

Bluetooth

The App uses Bluetooth only to find and connect to nearby DM3 displays and to exchange display settings and messages. That information goes only to your display. Demo mode works without Bluetooth permission.

Files

Importing and exporting message files and choosing a firmware update file access only the files and locations you choose.

Sharing with third parties and international transfers

Because the App does not collect personal information, no personal information is shared with third parties or transferred abroad.
